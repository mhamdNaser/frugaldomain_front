How it works

From Shopify to clarity in three steps

  1. 01

    Connect your store

    Create your Verdia account and add your store's myshopify domain with an Admin API access token. We verify the connection instantly and store the token encrypted.

    About 3 minutes

  2. 02

    We sync everything

    Verdia imports your catalog, orders, customers, inventory and content, then subscribes to Shopify webhooks so every change lands in your dashboard as it happens.

    Live from then on

  3. 03

    Run it from one place

    Browse, filter, export and edit. Changes you make in Verdia are queued and published back to Shopify, with every attempt tracked in the sync center.

    Two-way by design

Webhooks, verified

Shopify notifies Verdia the moment something changes. Every webhook is HMAC-verified before it is processed in a background queue.

Outbound sync you can audit

Edits you make are sent back to Shopify as tracked jobs. Each attempt is recorded, so failures are visible and can be retried.

Notified, not surprised

In-app notifications and a sync center keep you informed about imports, updates and anything that needs a second look.

Encrypted access tokens

Signed webhooks
